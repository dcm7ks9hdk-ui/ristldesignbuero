VAU2

Modular flat-pack trestle, assembled without tools.

Context
Design
Period
Summer 2026
Disciplines
Furniture design, Construction, Concept
Year
2026

conceived construction principle allows for tool-free assembly and disassembly, making it suitable for both temporary workspaces and permanent use.

Features:

Foldable – collapses in seconds for space-saving storage.
Flat-pack design – all components can be transported and stored flat.
Stackable – folded trestles stack neatly on top of one another.
Tool-free assembly and disassembly – quick setup and takedown without screws or additional tools.
Modular use – works as a classic trestle in pairs, or individually with a small tabletop as a compact side or work table.
The VAU2 unites clear construction with high flexibility, making it equally suited to work, exhibition, and living spaces.

This project was created in collaboration with Kilian Ritter (@ritter__studio).
